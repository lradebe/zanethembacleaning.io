Our Story

Hope, Dignity
& Transformation

Proudly Level 1 B-BBEE Contributor

Zanethemba, meaning "hope" in isiZulu, embodies our mission to transform lives through dignified employment and professional service excellence.

Founded in Boksburg, Gauteng, we emerged from a vision to create meaningful opportunities while delivering world-class cleaning solutions.

We believe in second chances, skill development, and community upliftment. Our team comprises dedicated professionals from diverse backgrounds, united by a commitment to excellence.

Every service we provide creates ripples of positive change—supporting families, building careers, and strengthening our community.
